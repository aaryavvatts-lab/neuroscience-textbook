# From Neuron to Bedside

![From Neuron to Bedside: an interactive neuroscience textbook for high school students and future doctors](img/cover.png){ width="100%" }

**A free, interactive neuroscience textbook for high school students and future doctors.**

Your brain has around 86 billion neurons.[^azevedo] Neurological conditions are now the leading cause of illness and disability in the world, affecting roughly 3.4 billion people.[^gbd] Even so, many high schools teach little or no neuroscience. I made this book to help with that.

!!! mascot-welcome "Hi, I'm Ply!"
    ![Ply the sea slug waving hello](img/mascot/welcome.png){ class="mascot-admonition-img" }
    I'm a sea slug, the same kind of animal scientists used to discover how memories form at a single synapse. I only have about 20,000 neurons, so I'm *very* curious about your 86 billion. Every big idea starts at a single synapse!

## What makes this book different

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-gesture-tap:{ .lg .middle } **Hands-on simulations**

    ---

    Every chapter has an interactive MicroSim. Fire an action potential, cut the visual pathway, block an artery, or build a night of sleep, all in your browser.

-   :material-stethoscope:{ .lg .middle } **A clinical case in every chapter**

    ---

    Each chapter ends **At the Bedside**: you follow a doctor's reasoning from symptoms to the exact part of the nervous system that's affected.

-   :material-trophy-outline:{ .lg .middle } **Built for the Brain Bee**

    ---

    It covers the topics that high school neuroscience competitions ask about, with practice questions, quizzes, and a mock exam. It also teaches the patient-diagnosis skills that some higher-level rounds include.

-   :material-lock-open-variant-outline:{ .lg .middle } **Free and open**

    ---

    You don't need an account, and there are no ads or paywalls. The book is licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, it works on a phone, and anyone can suggest a fix on GitHub.

</div>

## Start here

- **New to neuroscience?** Begin with [Chapter 1: Neurons, Glia, and the Nervous System](chapters/01-neurons-and-glia/index.md).
- **Preparing for the Brain Bee?** Read [How to Use This Book](how-to-use.md), then [Appendix A: Brain Bee Strategy and Mock Exam](appendices/brain-bee.md).
- **Thinking about medicine?** Jump to [Chapter 16: How Doctors Examine the Brain](chapters/16-how-doctors-examine-the-brain/index.md) to see where the whole book leads.
- **Want to play first?** Browse all the [interactive MicroSims](sims/index.md).

## What's inside

| | |
|---|---|
| **16 chapters** | From a single neuron to the hospital bedside |
| **16 interactive MicroSims** | Simulations you can control, plus an interactive learning graph |
| **A clinical case in every chapter** | Multiple sclerosis, Parkinson's disease, stroke, concussion, epilepsy, Alzheimer's disease, and more |
| **160 quiz questions** | Ten per chapter, each with an explanation |
| **A learning graph** | 253 concepts mapped so you learn prerequisites first |
| **A 50-question mock exam** | For Brain Bee practice, in Appendix A |
| **Glossary and FAQ** | 253 plain-language definitions and 75 common questions answered |
