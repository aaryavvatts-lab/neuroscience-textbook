# List of Chapters

Chapters 1 to 4 build the foundation. After that, you can read in order or follow your interests. Each chapter lists its prerequisites at the top.

1. **[Neurons, Glia, and the Nervous System](01-neurons-and-glia/index.md)**: What neurons and glia are, how the nervous system is organized, and why myelin matters (case: multiple sclerosis).
2. **[The Action Potential](02-action-potential/index.md)**: How ion gradients and voltage-gated channels create the electrical signal of the nervous system (case: local anesthetics).
3. **[Synapses and Neurotransmitters](03-synapses-and-neurotransmitters/index.md)**: How neurons talk chemically, the major neurotransmitters, and how drugs act at synapses (case: myasthenia gravis).
4. **[A Tour of the Brain: Neuroanatomy](04-neuroanatomy/index.md)**: The lobes, deep structures, brainstem, cerebellum, spinal cord, and cranial nerves (case: Phineas Gage).
5. **[Vision](05-vision/index.md)**: From photoreceptors to the visual cortex, and how lesions cause specific visual field losses (case: pituitary tumor).
6. **[Hearing, Touch, Taste, Smell, and Pain](06-hearing-touch-taste-smell-pain/index.md)**: The other senses, body maps in the cortex, and how pain works (case: phantom limb pain).
7. **[Movement: Muscles, Reflexes, and Motor Control](07-movement/index.md)**: Reflexes, motor cortex, basal ganglia, and cerebellum (case: Parkinson's disease).
8. **[The Developing and Teenage Brain](08-developing-teenage-brain/index.md)**: How the brain builds itself, prunes connections, and changes through adolescence (case: fetal alcohol spectrum disorder).
9. **[Learning, Memory, and Neuroplasticity](09-learning-memory-plasticity/index.md)**: Memory systems, the hippocampus, and how synapses change with learning (case: Patient H.M.).
10. **[Sleep and Brain States](10-sleep-and-brain-states/index.md)**: Circadian rhythms, sleep stages, EEG, and why teens need more sleep (case: narcolepsy).
11. **[Emotion, Stress, and the Body](11-emotion-and-stress/index.md)**: The amygdala, the stress response, and the HPA axis (case: post-traumatic stress disorder).
12. **[Language, Attention, and Decision-Making](12-language-attention-decisions/index.md)**: Language networks, attention, and how the brain makes choices (case: aphasia).
13. **[The Aging Brain and Neurodegeneration](13-aging-and-neurodegeneration/index.md)**: Normal aging vs disease, and new treatments for Alzheimer's and ALS (cases: Alzheimer's disease, ALS).
14. **[Brain Injury, Stroke, and Epilepsy](14-injury-stroke-epilepsy/index.md)**: Concussion, stroke warning signs, and seizure first aid (cases: concussion, stroke, first seizure).
15. **[Psychiatric Disorders, Developmental Disorders, and Addiction](15-psychiatric-disorders-and-addiction/index.md)**: Depression, anxiety, schizophrenia, autism, ADHD, and how addiction changes the brain (case: opioid overdose).
16. **[How Doctors Examine the Brain](16-how-doctors-examine-the-brain/index.md)**: The neurological exam, lesion localization, and brain imaging: the capstone (case: a full neurological workup).
