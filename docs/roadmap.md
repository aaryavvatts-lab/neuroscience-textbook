# Roadmap

This book is a living project. The plan below shows what is done and what is planned. Plans can change based on feedback from students and teachers, so if something here matters to you, [say so on GitHub](https://github.com/aaryavvatts-lab/neuroscience-textbook/issues/new/choose).

## Released

- **Version 1.0 (October 2026).** 16 chapters, 16 interactive MicroSims, a clinical case in every chapter, 160 quiz questions, a 253-concept learning graph, a glossary, an FAQ, a Brain Bee strategy guide with a 50-question mock exam, and a careers appendix.
- **Version 1.1 (October 2026).** A full science edit with an update of time-sensitive facts, a [For Teachers](teachers.md) page, a [Club and Study Group Kit](club-kit.md), printable chapter PDFs on the [Downloads](downloads.md) page, and a public [Accuracy and Corrections](accuracy.md) page.

## Planned

These are goals, not promises. Each one will be listed in the [changelog](https://github.com/aaryavvatts-lab/neuroscience-textbook/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) when it is done.

| Plan | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Expert review of every chapter** by neuroscientists, clinicians, and teachers, credited by name | Outside experts catch mistakes that authors miss |
| **A classroom and club pilot** that measures whether the chapters and simulations help students learn | To find out what actually works, using real data |
| **Spanish translations**, starting with Chapters 1 to 4, reviewed by fluent speakers | Brain Bee competitions run in many countries, and many students learn best in their first language |
| **Audio versions of each chapter** | For students who learn better by listening or who have visual impairments |
| **A Frontiers chapter** on brain-computer interfaces, gene therapies, and AI in neuroscience | These fields are changing fast, and students ask about them |
| **More simulations**, including an EEG reader and a neuron-firing lab | Hands-on practice for the skills used in higher Brain Bee rounds |
