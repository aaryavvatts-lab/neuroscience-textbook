# Concept Taxonomy

Every concept in the learning graph belongs to exactly one of these 12 categories. The category sets the color of the concept's node in the [learning graph viewer](../sims/graph-viewer/main.html). The categories run roughly in the order the book teaches them, from basic biology to the clinic.

| Code | Category | Description | Concepts | Color |
|------|----------|-------------|---------:|-------|
| `FOUND` | Foundational Biology and Chemistry | Basic biology and chemistry needed before anything else: cell membranes, ions, proteins, and concentration gradients. | 4 | SteelBlue |
| `CELL` | Neurons and Glia | The cells of the nervous system (neurons and glia), their parts, and how the nervous system is organized. | 19 | DarkGreen |
| `SIG` | Electrical and Chemical Signaling | How neurons send signals: membrane potentials, the action potential, synapses, receptors, and neurotransmitters. | 36 | Gold |
| `ANAT` | Neuroanatomy | The structures of the brain, spinal cord, and nerves, and how they are named, protected, and supplied with blood. | 23 | Teal |
| `SENS` | Sensory Systems and Pain | How the body turns light, sound, touch, taste, smell, and tissue damage into neural signals, and where those signals go. | 26 | Orange |
| `MOTOR` | Movement and Motor Control | Muscles, reflexes, the motor cortex, the basal ganglia, and the cerebellum. | 12 | DarkSlateBlue |
| `DEV` | Development and Aging | How the brain is built before birth, how it changes in childhood and the teenage years, and how it ages. | 14 | LimeGreen |
| `COG` | Learning, Memory, and Cognition | Learning, memory, neuroplasticity, language, attention, and decision-making. | 21 | DarkOrchid |
| `STATE` | Sleep, Emotion, and Stress | Consciousness, sleep and circadian rhythms, emotion, stress, and reward. | 23 | LightSkyBlue |
| `DIS` | Disorders and Diseases | Neurological, psychiatric, and developmental disorders, injuries, and addiction. | 39 | Crimson |
| `CLIN` | Clinical Exam, Imaging, and Treatment | How doctors examine, image, diagnose, and treat the nervous system, including drugs and procedures. | 25 | HotPink |
| `RES` | Research Methods and Careers | How neuroscience is done: classic cases and model organisms, research methods and ethics, new technology, and careers. | 11 | SaddleBrown |

**Total:** 253 concepts.

## Notes

- **Foundational Biology and Chemistry (FOUND)** is small on purpose. It holds only the few ideas from general biology and chemistry that the rest of the book builds on. *Nervous System* is also a starting point of the graph, but it is filed under Neurons and Glia (CELL).
- **Disorders and Diseases (DIS)** and **Clinical Exam, Imaging, and Treatment (CLIN)** appear in almost every chapter, because each chapter ends with a clinical case.
- See the [Taxonomy Distribution Report](taxonomy-distribution.md) for the full list of concepts in each category.
