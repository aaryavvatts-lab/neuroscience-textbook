# How to Use This Book

Every chapter follows the same pattern, so you always know where you are:

| Part of the chapter | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Hook** | Starts with a real-world question, often through Maya, a 16-year-old soccer player studying for the Brain Bee |
| **Core science** | Builds intuition with everyday analogies first, then the technical terms |
| **MicroSim** | An interactive simulation. *Don't skip these.* They're the fastest way to really understand an idea |
| **At the Bedside** | A fictional patient case: symptoms, then where the problem is, then diagnosis and treatment |
| **Common Misconceptions** | Popular brain myths, corrected |
| **Brain Bee Practice** | Five short-answer questions with hidden answers |
| **Exercises** | Twelve questions across all six levels of Bloom's Taxonomy |
| **Quiz** | Ten multiple-choice questions with explanations, on a separate page |

!!! mascot-tip "Ply's study tip"
    ![Ply with a lightbulb](img/mascot/tip.png){ class="mascot-admonition-img" }
    Try to answer a practice question *before* you open the answer. Struggling to recall something, then checking, makes memories stronger than rereading does. Chapter 9 explains why!

## Choose your path

=== "Curious reader"

    Read Chapters 1 to 4 in order. They're the foundation. After that, read the chapters you find most interesting. The [Learning Graph](learning-graph/index.md) shows which earlier ideas each chapter depends on.

=== "Brain Bee competitor"

    1. Read every chapter in order, and do each MicroSim.
    2. After each chapter, take the quiz and the Brain Bee Practice questions without looking.
    3. Keep a list of terms you miss, and review them in the [Glossary](glossary.md).
    4. Two weeks before your competition, take the mock exam in [Appendix A](appendices/brain-bee.md).
    5. Study Chapter 16 and every Bedside case for the diagnosis rounds.
    6. Check your own competition's official rules and study materials. They differ by chapter and year.

=== "Future doctor"

    Read Chapters 1 to 4, then go case by case: each chapter's **At the Bedside** section. Try the "Your turn" question before reading the answer. Finish with [Chapter 16](chapters/16-how-doctors-examine-the-brain/index.md), which pulls every case together into a full neurological workup.

=== "Teacher"

    Every MicroSim page has an embed code and a short lesson plan. Each chapter's quiz can be used as an exit ticket. All content is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, so you may share and adapt it for non-commercial teaching with attribution.

## Reading on a phone

Everything works on a phone. Simulations shrink to fit the screen. For the best simulation experience, use the **fullscreen** button under each one, or turn your phone sideways.
