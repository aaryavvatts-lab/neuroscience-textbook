# Changelog

All notable changes to *From Neuron to Bedside* are listed here. Corrections to facts are also listed publicly on the site's [Accuracy and Corrections](https://aaryavvatts-lab.github.io/neuroscience-textbook/accuracy/) page.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10

### Added
- **For Teachers** page with pacing plans, standards connections, and classroom routines.
- **Club and Brain Bee Study Group Kit** with a 10-session plan.
- **Downloads** page with printable PDF versions of every chapter for offline use.
- **Accuracy and Corrections** page describing how the book is checked and listing corrections.
- **Roadmap** page.
- GitHub issue forms for reporting mistakes, reporting classroom use, suggesting ideas, and volunteering as an expert reviewer.
- A pilot study kit in `Materials/pilot-study/` for measuring whether the book helps students learn.
- `EDITOR_DOMAIN.md`, an editorial brief for science reviewers.

### Changed
- A senior science edit of every chapter checked each claim again, updated time-sensitive statistics and approval statuses to the latest available as of October 2026, and added caveats where a simplification needed one.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-12

### Added
- First public release: 16 chapters, 16 interactive MicroSims, a clinical case in every chapter, 160 quiz questions, a 253-concept learning graph, a glossary, an FAQ, a Brain Bee strategy guide with a 50-question mock exam, and a careers appendix.
