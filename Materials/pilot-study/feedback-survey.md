## Notes for the study leader

- In `data-template.csv`, enter each rating as a number from 1 to 5 in columns `S1` to `S8`. Leave a cell blank if the student skipped it.
- Type the two open answers into a separate file, keyed only by ID number. Remove any names or identifying details while typing.
- Item S6 asks students to compare with "before." That is a self-report and is less reliable than the test scores. Report it as how students felt, not as proof of learning.
- If your review committee approves an online version, use a form tool with sign-in and email collection turned off.
